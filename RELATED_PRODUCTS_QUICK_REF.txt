HOW IT WORKS:
-------------
1. Product page loads → fetches product data
2. Once product data loads → fetches related products via server action
3. Related products are scored based on:
   - Same category (+10 points)
   - Matching keywords (+5 points each)
   - High ratings (+2 points for 4+ stars)
   - Has reviews (+1 point)
4. Top 8 scored products are returned and displayed in slider
5. Slider shows horizontal scrolling with nav buttons
6. Users can click any product to navigate

✓ Smart matching algorithm (category + keywords + ratings)

USAGE:
------
Automatic! Just visit any product page and scroll to bottom.
Example: /product/apache-flute

The "Related Products" section will appear with up to 8 related items.

TESTING:

2. Scroll to bottom

4. Click left/right arrows to scroll

6. Click any product to navigate

MATCHING LOGIC:
--------------
- Products in same category are prioritized
- Keywords from meta_keywords are compared
- Higher rated products get bonus points
- Products with reviews get bonus points
- Results sorted by relevance, then rating
- Maximum 8 products shown

CUSTOMIZATION:
-------------
To change number of products shown:
- Edit getRelatedProducts() call in page.tsx
- Change limit parameter (default: 8)

To change card width:
- Edit min-width/max-width in ProductCard styled component
- Adjust scrollAmount in scroll() function

To change scoring:
- Edit scoring logic in app/actions/related-products.ts
- Adjust point values for different criteria
